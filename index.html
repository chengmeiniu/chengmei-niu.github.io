About Me

I am a Ph.D. student at the School of Electronic Information and Communications, Huazhong University of Science and Technology (HUST), supervised by Prof. Zhenyu Liao. My background also includes an M.Sc. in Mathematics and Statistics from Chongqing University, and a B.E. in Economic Statistics from the School of Mathematics and Statistics, Ludong University.

Research Interests

  • Random Matrix Theory
  • Machine Learning
  • Randomized Numerical Linear Algebra

Projects

RIS-MIMO Coded Caching

Jointly optimizing channel reflection and caching to improve wireless system performance under spatial multiplexing.

Distributed Optimization

Designing efficient and communication-friendly Hessian-based Newton-type algorithms for large-scale machine learning.
